Frequently Asked

Common Questions

How do I contact Villa Mandhari in Diani Beach?

Villa Mandhari can be reached by telephone at +254 707 055 044, via email at info@villamandhari.com, or through WhatsApp for immediate assistance. Our dedicated concierge team is available daily from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM East Africa Time.

Where is Villa Mandhari located in Kenya?

Villa Mandhari is situated directly on the pristine white sand shores of Diani Beach, Kwale County, Kenya. Located approximately 35 kilometers south of Mombasa along the Indian Ocean coastline, the villa offers direct beach access and panoramic ocean views.

How can I make a reservation at Villa Mandhari?

Reservations can be arranged through WhatsApp at +254 707 055 044 for the quickest response, by telephone, or via email. We recommend booking well in advance, particularly during peak seasons: December through March and July through August.

Does Villa Mandhari offer airport transfers?

Yes, we offer private airport transfer services from Moi International Airport Mombasa (MBA) and Ukunda Airstrip (UKA). Our chauffeur service ensures a seamless, comfortable journey to your coastal sanctuary. Transfers can be arranged when confirming your reservation.
