East Africa's Premier Beachfront Villa Resort in Diani Beach, Kenya

Nestled along the pristine white sands of Diani Beach in Kwale County, Villa Mandhari stands as Kenya's most exclusive luxury villa resort—a sanctuary where contemporary elegance harmonizes with authentic coastal Swahili charm and world-class hospitality.

Our collection of 12 meticulously designed private villas, each offering unique architectural character and bespoke amenities, provides discerning travelers with an intimate beachfront retreat that transcends conventional luxury accommodation. From romantic honeymoon escapes to family celebrations and corporate retreats, Villa Mandhari delivers unforgettable experiences.

Good to Know

All 12 villas at Villa Mandhari are identical in layout and amenities. The only variation is in bedding configuration.

  • Request bedding at time of booking
  • Changes up to 48 hours before arrival
  • No additional charge for bed changes

Families Welcome

We love hosting families! Our villas are child-friendly with safety features throughout.

  • Baby cots available on request (no charge)
  • High chairs for dining
  • Child-proofing upon request
  • Kids' menu at our restaurant
